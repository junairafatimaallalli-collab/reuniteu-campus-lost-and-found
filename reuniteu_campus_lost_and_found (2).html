How ReuniteU Works

A simple, secure 5-step process designed to return belongings to their rightful owners efficiently.

1

Report

Post details of a lost item or something you found on campus.

2

Search

Browse live catalog with smart keyword & location filters.

3

Claim

Click "This is Mine" on matched listings to initiate verification.

4

Verify

Answer dynamic security questions to prove actual ownership.

5

Reunite

Coordinate pickup at campus security hub and mark reunited.
